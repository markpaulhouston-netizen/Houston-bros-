## Payment Terms

- **Deposit:** 50% due within 48 hours of contract signing to hold the date
- **Balance:** Remaining 50% due 30 days before event/check-in
- **Accepted Payments:** Credit card, ACH transfer, wire transfer, check (10+ days advance)
- **Cancellation Policy:**
  - 60+ days out: Full deposit refund
  - 30–59 days out: 50% of deposit refunded
  - Under 30 days: No refund; full balance may be due
  - Force Majeure: Handled case-by-case with owner approval
