## Sales Process

### Step 1 — Lead Capture (within 1 hour)
- Log all leads in the CRM immediately
- Record: name, contact info, event/stay type, dates, group size, budget (if known)
- Assign lead to the correct sales rep

### Step 2 — Initial Response (within 2 hours)
- Call or email the prospect
- Introduction script:
  > *"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from Houston Bros — thank you for reaching out! I'd love to learn more about what you're planning and make sure we find the perfect fit for you. Do you have a few minutes to chat?"*
- Goal: qualify the lead and schedule a discovery call or site visit

### Step 3 — Discovery & Qualification
Ask these questions:
- What is the occasion / event type?
- What dates are you considering?
- How many guests are you expecting?
- What is your approximate budget?
- Have you visited Houston Bros before?
- What's most important to you in a venue/stay?

Qualify using **BANT**: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline

### Step 4 — Proposal
- Send a personalized proposal within 24 hours of the discovery call
- Include: package options, pricing, availability, add-ons, testimonials
- Use the proposal template in `/sales/templates/`
- Follow up by phone within 48 hours if no response

### Step 5 — Handle Objections

| Objection                    | Response                                                              |
|------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| "It's too expensive"         | "Let me understand your budget — I may have a package that fits."    |
| "We're still looking around" | "That's smart! What would make Houston Bros the right choice?"       |
| "We need more time"          | "Absolutely — can I hold your date for 48 hours while you decide?"   |
| "The space is too small"     | "Tell me more about your vision — we have flexible configurations."  |
| "We had a bad experience"    | "I appreciate you sharing that. Let me show you what's changed."     |

### Step 6 — Close
- Ask for the business directly:
  > *"Based on everything we've discussed, I think [Package Name] is the perfect fit. Are you ready to move forward and secure your date?"*
- Offer a limited-time incentive if needed (complimentary upgrade, waived fee)
- All discounts over 15% require DOO approval

### Step 7 — Contract & Deposit
- Send digital contract within 24 hours of verbal agreement
- Require 50% deposit to hold the date
- Balance due 30 days before event / check-in
- Use DocuSign or equivalent for all contracts

### Step 8 — Handoff to Operations
- Brief the operations team 72 hours before the event/stay
- Share: guest name, package details, special requests, VIP notes
- Update CRM record to "Confirmed — Operations Briefed"

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Always listen more than you talk
- Know the packages and pricing inside out
- Follow up every lead at least 3 times before marking as lost
- Update CRM after every single interaction
- Celebrate wins with the team

**Don't:**
- Discount without DOO approval
- Promise something you can't deliver
- Let a lead go more than 48 hours without follow-up
- Skip the CRM — every interaction must be logged
- Take a "no" personally — ask for feedback instead
