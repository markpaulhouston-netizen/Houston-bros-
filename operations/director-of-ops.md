# Director of Operations — Role & Playbook
## Houston Bros Hospitality

---

## Role Overview

The **Director of Operations (DOO)** is the operational backbone of Houston Bros. This role bridges executive vision with day-to-day execution across all departments — ensuring the business runs smoothly, profitably, and with an exceptional guest experience at every touchpoint.

**Reports to:** Owner / Executive Team  
**Manages:** Marketing Manager, Sales Manager, CRM/Guest Relations Lead, Front-of-House Supervisor, Event Coordinators

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Daily Operations Management
- Open and close operational checklists reviewed each shift
- Monitor occupancy, bookings, and revenue dashboards
- Resolve escalated guest complaints within 2 hours
- Conduct daily team stand-ups (10 minutes, every morning at 9 AM)
- Audit inventory, supplies, and vendor delivery schedules

### 2. Team Leadership & Accountability
- Set weekly priorities for each department head
- Conduct 1-on-1 check-ins with department leads (weekly)
- Full team performance reviews (quarterly)
- Hire, onboard, and coach new staff using the [SOPs](standard-operating-procedures.md)
- Maintain a culture of hospitality, professionalism, and accountability

### 3. Financial Oversight
- Review daily P&L and flag variances above 5%
- Approve expenses up to $2,500 independently
- Monthly budget reviews with ownership
- Track KPIs: RevPAR, ADR, occupancy rate, guest satisfaction score (GSS)
- Identify and act on cost-reduction opportunities without sacrificing service quality

### 4. Marketing Oversight
- Review and approve all marketing content before publication
- Attend monthly marketing strategy sessions
- Ensure brand consistency across all channels
- Hold marketing accountable to lead generation targets
- See full detail: [`../marketing/marketing-strategy.md`](../marketing/marketing-strategy.md)

### 5. Sales Oversight
- Review sales pipeline weekly (every Monday, 11 AM)
- Approve discounts and special packages above 15%
- Attend major client proposals and closing meetings when required
- Monitor conversion rate targets (goal: ≥ 30% lead-to-booking)
- See full detail: [`../sales/sales-playbook.md`](../sales/sales-playbook.md)

### 6. CRM & Guest Relations
- Ensure all client records are current in the CRM within 24 hours of interaction
- Review VIP guest lists before each week's events/stays
- Champion guest feedback loops (surveys, Google Reviews, direct outreach)
- Target: Net Promoter Score (NPS) ≥ 70
- See full detail: [`../crm/crm-guide.md`](../crm/crm-guide.md)

## 30 / 60 / 90-Day Onboarding Plan

### First 30 Days — Learn
- Shadow every department for at least one full day
- Read all SOPs, marketing materials, and sales decks
- Meet every team member and key vendor
- Review last 12 months of financial reports
- Audit the CRM for completeness and accuracy

### Days 31–60 — Assess
- Identify the top 3 operational bottlenecks
- Propose improvements to the owner with a business case
- Establish your weekly rhythm and communication cadence
- Launch at least one marketing or sales initiative

### Days 61–90 — Execute
- Implement approved operational improvements
- Hit KPI targets for 8 of the 10 tracked metrics
- Deliver first formal performance reviews to department leads
- Present a 90-day results report and Q2 roadmap to ownership
