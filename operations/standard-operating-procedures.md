# Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
## Houston Bros Hospitality

---

## 1. Guest Check-In / Check-Out

### Check-In
1. Greet guest by name and with a smile within 30 seconds of arrival.
2. Confirm reservation details (name, dates, room/package).
3. Verify photo ID and payment method on file.
4. Explain property amenities, house rules, and emergency exits.
5. Provide room key / access code and escort if a VIP booking.
6. Log check-in time in the CRM immediately.

### Check-Out
1. Ask about guest experience — note any feedback in the CRM.
2. Review charges, process final payment, and provide receipt.
3. Request a Google Review (QR code at desk).
4. Mark guest record as "Checked Out" and flag for post-stay follow-up email (24 hrs).

---

## 2. Handling Guest Complaints

1. **Listen** without interrupting. Acknowledge the issue.
2. **Apologize** sincerely, regardless of fault.
3. **Act** — resolve on the spot if possible (comp, upgrade, refund).
4. **Escalate** to DOO if unresolved within 30 minutes.
5. **Document** every complaint in the CRM under the guest record.
6. **Follow up** within 24 hours to confirm satisfaction.

---

## 3. New Booking Intake

1. Receive inquiry (phone, email, website, social media).
2. Log lead in CRM within 1 hour of receipt.
3. Send initial response within 2 hours with availability and pricing.
4. Follow sales playbook for qualification and close.
5. Upon confirmation: send contract, collect deposit, create event/reservation record.
6. Brief operations team 72 hours before guest arrival.

---

## 4. Vendor Management

1. Maintain an approved vendor list (updated quarterly).
2. All new vendors require DOO approval before first use.
3. Minimum two quotes for any purchase over $500.
4. Purchase orders required for all vendor payments.
5. Review vendor performance at quarterly operations meeting.

---

## 5. Staff Scheduling

1. Schedules posted by Thursday for the following week.
2. Minimum 48 hours notice required for shift changes.
3. DOO approves all overtime in advance.
4. Coverage plan required for all holidays and peak periods (30+ days in advance).

---

## 6. Emergency Procedures

- **Fire:** Activate alarm, evacuate all guests, call 911, notify DOO.
- **Medical:** Call 911 first, administer first aid (certified staff only), notify DOO.
- **Security Incident:** Call security / 911, document incident, notify DOO immediately.
- **Power Outage:** Activate emergency lighting, contact utility provider, notify DOO.

All incidents must be documented in the incident log within 2 hours.
