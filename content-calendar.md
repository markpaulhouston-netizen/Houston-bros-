## Workflow

Each scheduled slot is fed by:
1. A caption from `captions/<venue>.md`
2. A media asset (real photo to start; AI video later)
3. The account ID from `automation/blotato-accounts.md`

The recurring n8n workflow (see `automation/n8n-workflow.md`) drafts each
week's slots automatically for human approval before they post.
