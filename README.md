A complete business operations platform for **Houston Bros**, covering Director of Operations oversight, Marketing, Sales, and Customer Relationship Management (CRM).

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Director of Operations** — Start with [`operations/director-of-ops.md`](operations/director-of-ops.md) for the full leadership playbook.
2. **Marketing** — Review [`marketing/marketing-strategy.md`](marketing/marketing-strategy.md) and assign campaign owners.
3. **Sales** — Follow the [`sales/sales-playbook.md`](sales/sales-playbook.md) to convert leads into bookings.
4. **CRM** — Onboard clients using [`crm/crm-guide.md`](crm/crm-guide.md) and track every relationship.
5. **Dashboard** — Open [`dashboard/index.html`](dashboard/index.html) in a browser for a live operations overview.

- ✅ Revenue Growth (Sales + Marketing)
