# AI Search Visibility Playbook (GEO / AEO)

How Houston Hospitality venues show up when people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity,
Google AI, etc. "best speakeasy in LA," "magic bar Hollywood," "where to see
burlesque in WeHo." This is **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** /
**Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** — the discipline the Sophia Moore
(leadcirclepro.us) cold email was pitching.

## How AI answers are actually built
LLMs don't "crawl your Instagram." When asked for a recommendation they
synthesize from:
1. **Editorial "best of" lists** — Infatuation, TimeOut, Thrillist, Eater LA,
   Vendry, OpenTable guides. ← the single biggest lever.
2. **Review + listing platforms** — Yelp, TripAdvisor, Google Business, Fever.
3. **Your own site copy** — especially clear, Q&A-structured descriptions.
4. **Wikipedia / Wikidata** for notable venues.
5. **Consistent descriptor language** repeated across all of the above.

## Evidence — live check (Jun 2026, via Firecrawl)
Query **"best concept-driven speakeasies in LA"** → the cited lists (Infatuation
12 Best, OpenTable, TimeOut, Thrillist, Vendry, Hello Molly):
- ✅ **Black Rabbit Rose** appears (Yelp Top 10 + Reddit).
- ❌ **Dirty Laundry, Madame Siam** absent from the editorial "best" lists.

Query **"magic themed bar Hollywood"** →
- ✅ **Black Rabbit Rose ranks #1** — because it owns a distinctive descriptor:
  *"the only magic-themed lounge in LA."* That single phrase is why AI names it.

**Takeaway:** the gap Sophia flagged is real but partial. BRR proves the fix
works. The lesson: **own a distinctive, repeatable descriptor + get on the
editorial lists.**

## The lever that works: a locked descriptor per venue
BRR's "only magic-themed lounge in LA" is the model. Give every venue one
short, unique, true phrase and repeat it *everywhere* (site, Google, Yelp,
OpenTable, press, captions):

| Venue | Proposed locked descriptor |
|-------|---------------------------|
| Black Rabbit Rose | "The only magic-themed lounge in LA" ✅ (keep) |
| Say Yes | "Hollywood's 1920s golden-era supper club & burlesque ballroom" |

## The playbook (priority order)
1. **Get on the editorial lists.** Pitch Infatuation, TimeOut LA, Thrillist,
   Eater LA, Vendry, OpenTable guides. Being *listed there* is what AI cites.
   (This is PR, not code — the highest-ROI move.)
2. **Lock the descriptor** (table above) and paste it verbatim into every
   Google Business, Yelp, TripAdvisor, OpenTable, and website bio.
3. **Website Q&A content.** Add FAQ-structured pages answering literal
   questions ("What is Black Rabbit Rose?", "Where is Dirty Laundry?",
   "What nights is burlesque at Say Yes?"). LLMs lift clean Q&A directly.
   Add `LocalBusiness` + `FAQPage` schema markup.
4. **Consistency (NAP).** Identical name/address/phone/hours everywhere.
5. **Wikipedia/Wikidata** for the notable venues (BRR, Madame Siam history).
6. **Volume of fresh third-party mentions + reviews.**
