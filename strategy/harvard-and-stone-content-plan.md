## Four patterns to apply everywhere
1. **Name the performer/DJ in every post** — never a faceless flyer.
2. **Recurring named theme nights** = appointment viewing.
3. **Urgency + playful hooks** — "Low ticket alert," a curiosity opener.
4. **Mystique as a flex** — the factory, the fireplace, the lore.

## Night-by-night plan (mapped to H&S programming)

### Monday — Pool Competition (the $1,000 arc)
- **Format:** weekly "This week at H&S" roundup reel (Gold Diggers model) +
  the running pool-tally graphic.
- **Hook:** "Week ___. Pot's at $___. Think you can run the table?"
- **Asset:** short reel of last week's winning shot + bracket update.

### Tuesday — Taco, Tequila, Tattoo
- **Format:** food-truck-of-the-week feature (no kitchen on site — make the
  truck the star), tattoo close-ups, tequila pour.
- **Hook:** "Tacos. Tequila. Fresh ink. Name a better Tuesday. 🌮"
- **Steal from:** Desert 5 Spot's themed-night identity consistency.

### Thursday — All Girls All Vinyl
- **Format:** performer-first DJ spotlight (Zebulon model). Name her, tag her,
  shoot the needle drop and the balcony-from-below angle.
- **Hook:** "All girls. All vinyl. Tonight on the decks: @____ 🎶 Low cover, high energy."

### Fri/Sat — Coyote Ugly-style Burlesque
- **Format:** high-energy bar-top POV reels, crowd reaction, performer hype.
- **Hook:** "The bar IS the stage. 🥃🔥" + name the performers.
- **Mystique layer:** the 1940s factory + fireplace as the cinematic backdrop.

### Sunday — Soda Pop Sundays
- **Format:** nostalgic, colorful, lighter palette; family-cool crossover.
- **Hook:** "Soda Pop Sundays. Sweet tooth, worst decisions. 🍒"

## Cadence target
Stop one-off flyers. Run each night as a **named, repeatable franchise** with a
performer named every post and a Monday roundup anchoring the week — exactly how
Zebulon and Gold Diggers compound their audiences.
