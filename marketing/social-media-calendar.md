## Monthly Content Themes

### January — "Fresh Start, New Experiences"
- Promote Valentine's Day packages (early bird)
- New Year, new stays — showcase refreshed spaces
- Feature: guest resolutions and goals (interactive)

### February — "Love & Luxury"
- Valentine's Day campaign (packages, romantic getaway)
- Galentine's / Friends' Weekend promotions
- Feature: couples stories and testimonials

### March — "Spring Has Arrived"
- Spring staycation push
- Showcase outdoor spaces, pools, patios
- Feature: staff spotlight series begins

### April — "Houston in Bloom"
- Local Houston events tie-ins (Houston Livestock Show aftermath, spring festivals)
- Easter/spring break family packages
- Feature: behind-the-scenes property tour

### May — "Memorial Day & Summer Kickoff"
- Summer staycation launch campaign
- Memorial Day special offers
- Feature: "Top 5 things to do in Houston this summer"

### June — "Summer Vibes"
- Mid-year review deals for corporate clients
- Pool + entertainment content
- Feature: guest experience highlight reel

### July — "Peak Season"
- Last-minute summer deals
- Fourth of July event content
- Feature: team culture and values post

### August — "Back to Business"
- Corporate retreat + team building campaign
- "End of summer" last-chance offers
- Feature: partnership spotlight

### September — "Fall into Houston Bros"
- Fall décor and ambiance content
- Corporate holiday party booking push begins
- Feature: local Houston culture/food tie-in

### October — "Holiday Season Begins"
- Halloween event promotions
- Holiday party packages launch
- Feature: testimonials + reviews highlight

### November — "Gratitude & Gathering"
- Thanksgiving private event promotions
- "What We're Thankful For" brand story
- Feature: team appreciation post

### December — "Celebrate in Style"
- New Year's Eve event campaign
- Christmas gift package promotions
- Year-in-review content (photos, milestones, thanks)

> [Property photo or team photo]

### Tuesday — Tip or How-To
> *"Planning a corporate event in Houston? Here are 3 things to look for in a venue..."*

### Wednesday — Guest Spotlight / Testimonial
> *"We loved having [Guest Name] with us last weekend! Here's what they had to say..."*

### Thursday — Offer / Promotion
> *"🔥 Limited-time weekend package — book by Friday and save 20%!"*

### Friday — Behind the Scenes / Culture
> *"It's Friday at Houston Bros — here's a peek at how we set the stage for a perfect weekend 👀"*

### Saturday — Event / Activity Highlight
> *"This is what your Saturday could look like... 🌟"*

### Sunday — Reflection / Engagement
> *"What's your favorite thing about staying with us? Drop a comment below 👇"*
