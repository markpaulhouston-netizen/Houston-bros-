# Marketing Strategy
## Houston Bros Hospitality

---

## Brand Identity

**Brand Voice:** Warm, professional, aspirational, local pride  
**Target Audience:**
- Corporate clients (events, retreats, team stays)
- Leisure travelers (couples, families, influencers)
- Event planners and wedding coordinators
- Local community partnerships

**Unique Selling Proposition (USP):**  
*"Houston Bros delivers a hospitality experience rooted in authentic Houston culture — where every guest feels like family."*

## Monthly Marketing Calendar

### Week 1 — Brand & Awareness
- Post 3 lifestyle/property showcase posts (Instagram + Facebook)
- Send bi-weekly email newsletter (promotions + upcoming events)
- Update Google Business profile (photos, posts, offers)

### Week 2 — Engagement & Community
- Run 1 interactive campaign (poll, contest, Q&A)
- Respond to all reviews within 24 hours (Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor)
- Share 1 local Houston culture story or partnership spotlight

### Week 3 — Lead Generation
- Launch or optimize 1 paid ad campaign (Google or Meta)
- Publish 1 blog post or SEO article (Google ranking + email)
- Promote a limited-time offer or package

### Week 4 — Retention & Conversion
- Follow-up email to past guests with a re-booking offer
- Share guest testimonials and user-generated content
- Review analytics and adjust strategy for next month

---

## Campaign Types

### 1. Seasonal Campaigns
- Valentine's Day Getaway Package (January launch)
- Summer Staycation (May launch)
- Holiday Party Packages (October launch)
- New Year's Eve Event (November launch)

### 2. Corporate / B2B Campaigns
- "Book Your Next Team Retreat" LinkedIn + email outreach
- Corporate rate program with dedicated booking contact

### 3. Influencer & Partnership Campaigns
- Host 1–2 micro-influencers per month for a complimentary stay
- Co-marketing with local Houston restaurants, venues, and brands

### 4. Loyalty & Referral Campaigns
- Guest referral program: $50 credit for each successful referral
- Loyalty tier program (Bronze / Silver / Gold)
